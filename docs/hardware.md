## OLED

The target display is an SSD1306 I2C OLED, expected to use VCC, GND, SDA and SCL.

For a standard Arduino Uno, the conventional I2C pins are SDA -> A4 and SCL -> A5.

Do not connect the display until the exact module labels and voltage requirements have been confirmed from the component photos.
